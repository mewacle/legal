Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mewacle, a cat-themed oracle card reading app for entertainment and motivational purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

By using Mewacle, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Automatically collected when you submit feedback:

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 16 Pro)
  • Operating system and version (e.g. iOS 18.1)
  • Country (derived from your device locale)
  • Timezone (e.g. America/New_York)
  • Language setting (e.g. en-US)
  • App version

Provided voluntarily:

  • Satisfaction rating (1–5 emoji scale)
  • Optional written comment

We do NOT collect: your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos, payment information, or health data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Understand how users engage with the app
  • Improve the quality of card readings and content
  • Fix bugs and improve app performance
  • Inform future feature development

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Airtable — Feedback responses are stored securely in Airtable. Airtable Privacy Policy

Google AdMob — Our app displays ads via Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier. You can limit ad tracking in your device Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google Privacy Policy

Expo / React Native — The app is built using Expo, which may collect anonymised crash data. Expo Privacy Policy

5. Push Notifications

With your permission, Mewacle sends daily push notifications to remind you to draw your daily card. These are scheduled locally on your device and do not transmit personal data.

You can disable notifications anytime in the app (Menu → Settings) or in your device Settings → Notifications → Mewacle.

6. Data Storage & Security

Feedback data is stored in Airtable and is accessible only to the Mewacle development team. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access.

7. Data Retention

Feedback data is retained for as long as necessary to improve the app. You may request deletion of any feedback you have submitted by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Mewacle is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance.
